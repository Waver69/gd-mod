# insirius's mod menu

A mod menu for Geometry Dash on PC and Android.

- **Input buffer with a path predictor.** It simulates each click with GD's own physics and holds back clicks that would kill you until they're safe.
- **Player:** noclip, jump hack, hitboxes, path prediction, no death effect, no respawn flash, no mirror, no camera shake.
- **Global:** speedhack (with audio), FPS unlock, no particles, instant transitions, hide pause menu.
- **Bypass:** safe mode, unlock icons/items/shops/vaults/treasure room/main levels, custom object, slider, character filter, text length, checkpoint limit, verify.
- **Level:** accurate percentage, auto pickup coins, auto practice mode.
- **Macro:** record and replay inputs. Practice checkpoints are handled.
- **Tools:** autoclicker, frame stepper.
- **Labels:** cheat indicator, FPS, CPS, percent, attempts, noclip accuracy and more.

To open the menu, press **Tab** on PC, or tap the **insirius** button on your phone.
